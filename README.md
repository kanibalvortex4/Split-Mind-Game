## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

*Split Mind* is an engaging first-person narrative-driven game available on **Windows** that explores the complexities of living with schizophrenia. The latest version immerses players in a gripping four-day experience where daily tasks become increasingly challenging as psychosis intensifies. Players must manage their fear through breathing techniques while navigating a world filled with emotional depth and personal struggles.

This powerful exploration of mental health aims to foster empathy and understanding of the feelings and stigma associated with schizophrenia. The game's unique blend of strategy and storytelling encourages players to engage deeply with critical social issues, making it a must-play for anyone interested in narrative-driven experiences.
